# Homepage direction

The homepage introduces recorded-signal investigation; the workspace remains the
working instrument. The opening uses ink navy (#172740), cool white (#F5F7FA),
signal blue (#4269E1), muted slate (#91A2BB), and a restrained gold playhead accent
(#E6B35F). Manrope carries all typography, with a large, compact headline and quiet
technical captions. The existing real product screenshot is the hero asset. It
shows a bundled synthetic recording and is explicitly identified as such.

Sequence: clear promise and workspace CTA → actual product preview → three-step
investigation workflow → supported formats and explicit field contracts → native
FAQ with accurate limits → final workspace CTA and source links. Desktop uses a
wide product stage; mobile stacks content without hiding the core explanation.

GSAP introduces heading words, supporting copy and product stage, then gives the
workflow a restrained section entrance. Lenis is the only smooth-scroll engine;
it fits the native anchor navigation better than Locomotive. Reduced motion
skips initialization. No Three.js, decorative shader, stock photo, illustration,
customer logo, invented metric, authentication mock, or pricing claim is needed.
Manrope, Solar icons and existing vendored motion libraries retain their licenses.
The preview JPEG is copied unchanged from the previously verified app screenshot.

Frontend-design and build-awwwards-quality-sites guide the composition and motion.
Karpathy guidelines keep the working tool intact and all homepage claims tied to
implemented functionality. Browser verification covers home → workspace and the
responsive/keyboard/reduced-motion states; backend/cloud work is separate.
