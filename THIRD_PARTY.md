# Asset provenance

All fonts and browser libraries are served locally. No analytics, media CDN,
remote model endpoint, imagery service, or icon runtime is required.

| Asset | Package | Version | Terms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| GSAP and ScrollTrigger | `gsap` | 3.15.0 | [GSAP Standard License](https://gsap.com/standard-license/); original notices preserved |
| Lenis | `lenis` | 1.3.27 | MIT; bundled license |
| Solar icons | `@iconify-json/solar` | 1.2.13 | Solar by [480 Design](https://www.figma.com/community/file/1166831539721848736), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), through [Iconify](https://iconify.design/) |

Icons embedded in `index.html`: add-square-linear, folder-with-files-linear,
download-linear, play-circle-linear, restart-linear, and skip-next-linear. Their
size and color adapt to the interface. The small `er` mark is original. Waveforms
are data graphics derived from imported values. The sample drive and CAN files
are deterministic synthetic fixtures included to demonstrate the workflow; they
are not recordings from a real vehicle. No stock imagery, portraits, testimonials,
partner logos, or endorsement claims appear in the app.
