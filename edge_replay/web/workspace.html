CAN decoding and timestamp rules

The included driver profile reads two little-endian IEEE 754 floats per eight-byte frame: 0x300 = throttle variation + brake usage (%), 0x301 = energy (Wh/km) + regen efficiency (0..1), 0x302 = jerk (m/s³) + padding. Unknown IDs and malformed payloads are rejected. CAN timestamps may be equal; source order is retained. Signal timestamps must strictly increase. A frame's update does not mean every channel is fresh.
